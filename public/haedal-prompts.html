해님 달님 삽화 프롬프트

20쪽 · 평면 벡터 그림체 · 1584×672 가로형. 머리말을 앞에 한 번만 두고 SECTION 1~20으로 이어 붙였습니다.

Draw 20 illustrations for a Korean children's picture book: THE SUN AND THE MOON (Haenim Dalnim / the brother and sister who became sun and moon).
Each SECTION below is one page. Keep the style and the character designs identical across all 20.

STYLE (apply identically to every section): FLAT VECTOR illustration. Completely flat color fields — no gradients, no soft shading, no 3D rendering, no texture. Bold simple shapes with clean edges. Layered flat hills in two or three greens. Simple rounded white cloud shapes. Cute minimal faces: small dot or oval eyes, tiny rosy oval cheeks, simple curved smiles, no nose or a tiny dot. Warm limited palette — deep forest green, tan and warm brown, cream, coral pink, indigo blue; night scenes use a deep navy sky with a yellow crescent moon and small yellow stars.

CANVAS: wide panoramic, aspect ratio 2.36:1, 1584x672. Compose for a LONG HORIZONTAL frame — spread the scene sideways, never crop a tall composition.

CAST (keep these designs identical in every section):
- MOTHER — white jeogori (short jacket) over a long indigo-blue chima (skirt), black hair in a low bun, round kind face with rosy cheeks.
- BROTHER (older) — green jeogori, cream trousers, black bowl-cut hair, cheerful round face.
- SISTER (younger, smaller) — coral-pink hanbok, black bob with straight bangs, round face.
- TIGER — orange body with black stripes, cream muzzle and belly, the Chinese-style king mark on the forehead drawn as simple black brush strokes, long striped tail, big round yellow eyes. From SECTION 7 onward he wears the MOTHER'S white jeogori and indigo chima as a disguise, but his striped tail, paws and face stay clearly tiger.
- RICE-CAKE BASKET — round woven straw basket holding white round rice cakes.
- SKY KING (only in SECTIONS 17-19) — kind old man with a long white beard, tall dark hat, flowing pale-blue robe, standing on clouds.

RULES for every section:
(1) NO text anywhere — no letters, no Korean or Chinese characters, no numbers, no speech bubbles, no signature, no watermark. (The tiger's forehead mark is decorative brush strokes only.)
(2) Never draw the same character twice in one image. No duplicate figures.
(3) The art must bleed to all four edges — no white margin, no rounded frame, no border.
(4) The lower quarter must be fully painted, never left blank.
(5) Wholesome and child-friendly. No blood, no wounds, no visible violence — the tiger is comic and a little silly, never gruesome. Do not show the mother being harmed.
(6) Only the characters named in that section appear. Do not add villagers or extra people.

============================================================

SECTION 1
Daytime. A small thatched-roof cottage stands at the foot of layered green mountains. In front of it MOTHER stands holding BROTHER's hand on one side and SISTER's hand on the other, all three smiling. The woven basket of white rice cakes sits on the ground beside them. Blue sky, simple white clouds.

SECTION 2
Morning. MOTHER walks away along a winding dirt path that snakes up over layered green hills, the rice-cake basket balanced on her head, one hand steadying it, looking back over her shoulder. Far behind her, the small thatched cottage with its wooden door closed. No children in frame.

SECTION 3
Interior of the small room at dusk, seen from inside. The wooden door is shut and barred. BROTHER and SISTER sit close together on the floor, SISTER pressed against her brother's side, both looking toward the door. A single small lamp glows. The paper window shows deep orange sunset outside. Only the two children.

SECTION 4
A hilltop path at sunset, orange and pink sky. The TIGER stands upright blocking the path, one paw raised, mouth open in a roar. MOTHER, basket on her head, leans back startled and tosses one white rice cake toward him with her free hand. Long horizontal composition with hills stretching away.

SECTION 5
A wide panoramic view of three or four hill ridges receding into the distance, drawn flat and layered. On the near ridge MOTHER walks with her basket, now clearly emptier. The TIGER's striped shape appears again on the next ridge, and once more smaller on the ridge beyond — showing he keeps returning. Deepening evening colors.

SECTION 6
The last hilltop at night, deep navy sky with a yellow crescent moon. MOTHER holds the basket upside down — completely empty — with both hands, shoulders drooping. The TIGER looms in front of her, upright, paws spread wide, grinning. Keep it non-violent: no contact, no harm shown, just the confrontation.

SECTION 7
Night at the cottage. The TIGER now wears the MOTHER'S white jeogori and indigo chima, standing at the wooden door and knocking with one striped paw — his tail and tiger face plainly visible. Inside, seen through the open sliding paper doors, BROTHER and SISTER stand together looking puzzled, heads tilted. Warm lamplight inside, navy night outside.

SECTION 8
Close, wide view of the wooden door from inside the room. A furry orange striped paw pokes through the gap at the edge of the door. BROTHER and SISTER recoil, clutching each other's hands tightly, eyes wide with fright. Warm lamp light, strong flat shapes.

SECTION 9
Night courtyard, wide panoramic. On the left the cottage door bulges as it is shaken. In the middle BROTHER and SISTER run hand in hand across the yard on tiptoe. On the right a big tree stands beside a round stone well, and the children are already climbing its trunk. Deep navy sky, crescent moon, flat silhouetted grass.

SECTION 10
The TIGER (still in the hanbok disguise) leans over the round stone well, pointing down at the water where two small reflections of the children appear. Above and to the side, BROTHER and SISTER sit on a thick tree branch; SISTER has both hands over her mouth, laughing. Night, crescent moon.

SECTION 11
The TIGER stands at the foot of the tree looking straight up, both paws raised questioningly. High on the branch BROTHER leans down calling out, one hand cupped beside his mouth, a sly clever smile. SISTER peeks out from behind him. Night sky with stars.

SECTION 12
The TIGER hugs the tree trunk and slides down it, legs splayed, paws shiny with oil, a comic look of dismay on his face. A small overturned oil jar lies at the base of the tree. Above, SISTER is mid-shout with one arm out and BROTHER claps a hand to his forehead. Funny, not scary.

SECTION 13
The TIGER swings an axe into the tree trunk, chips flying, climbing as he goes. The tree leans and its branches sway. At the very top BROTHER and SISTER cling to the last branch, nowhere left to climb, SISTER's mouth open in a cry. Night, tense but not gruesome — no blood, no wound.

SECTION 14
At the very top of the tree. BROTHER and SISTER press their palms together and look up into a wide starry navy sky, faces earnest and hopeful. The tree top occupies the lower right; the rest of the long frame is open night sky with a crescent moon and scattered yellow stars.

SECTION 15
A thick shining rope descends from a break of light in the night sky. BROTHER and SISTER grip it and are lifted, floating up above the treetop, hair and clothes drifting. Far below at the base of the tree the TIGER stamps his feet and reaches up with both paws. Long vertical motion across a wide horizontal frame.

SECTION 16
A second rope, visibly frayed and rotted, snaps in mid-air. The TIGER tumbles down head over heels, legs in the air, a comic surprised face. Below him spreads a field of tall sorghum stalks whose stems are red. No blood and no impact shown — he is still falling. Night sky above, red-stemmed field below.

SECTION 17
Above the clouds. The SKY KING with a long white beard, tall dark hat and pale-blue robe stands on a bank of flat white clouds, one hand extended in welcome. BROTHER and SISTER stand before him, BROTHER speaking with one hand on his chest. Soft daylight sky, flat cloud shapes across the wide frame.

SECTION 18
Above the clouds. MOTHER descends gently on a shaft of light, arms opening wide. BROTHER and SISTER run to her and all three embrace in the middle of the frame. The SKY KING stands to one side with one hand still raised, smiling. Warm golden light spreading across the clouds.

SECTION 19
Above the clouds, wide symmetrical composition. On the left a large flat golden sun disc, on the right a large pale crescent moon. SISTER stands before the sun and BROTHER before the moon, and they are reaching across to each other, hands nearly touching — the moment of swapping. BROTHER smiles warmly at his little sister. The SKY KING watches from behind.

SECTION 20
Final wide panorama split left to right. LEFT: bright daytime — a golden sun with SISTER's gentle smiling face shining over green hills and the little thatched cottage. RIGHT: night — a pale crescent moon with BROTHER's warm smiling face over the same hills, now dark blue with stars. Between them a band of soft pink-orange sunset in which the MOTHER'S kind face appears faintly in the clouds, watching over both.
